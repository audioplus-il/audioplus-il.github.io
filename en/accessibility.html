Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Aspire Home Audio Plus Ltd believes in making this website accessible to all users, including people with disabilities, and works to give everyone an equal and convenient browsing experience.

Accessibility level

This site was built in accordance with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 2013, and with Israeli Standard 5568, which is based on the WCAG 2.0 guidelines at level AA.

Adjustments made on this site

  • Semantic page structure, with hierarchical headings and defined page regions.
  • Full keyboard navigation, with a clear indicator of the active element.
  • A "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Alternative text for all images.
  • Color contrast that meets the ratio required by the standard.
  • Support for browser text enlargement up to 200% without loss of content.
  • Adaptation to different screens, including mobile phones.
  • The page language is set, and segments in another language are marked, for screen readers.
  • The user's reduced-motion preference is respected.
  • A built-in accessibility menu: high contrast, monochrome, link highlighting, readable font, and text enlargement and reduction.

Known limitations

This site is a simple landing page. The award image is a picture, and the text engraved in it also appears in its alternative text. If you run into any difficulty, we would be glad to hear about it and fix it.
